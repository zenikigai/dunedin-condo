Property & lifestyle

A ground-floor end unit near the water with everyday conveniences close by.

This 1BR / 1BA condo is a clean, quiet ground-floor end unit overlooking a park-like setting. The home has mostly tile flooring, newer bedroom flooring, and assigned parking close to the entrance.

Enjoy the private community beach and waterfront parks for sunsets, kayaking, BBQs, and time by the water. The community also offers a heated pool, waterfront clubhouse, fitness center, tennis, and additional shared amenities.

The Pinellas Trail is directly across the street, with convenient access to downtown Dunedin, Publix, Walgreens, restaurants, and coffee shops—waterfront living without needing to cross bridges for everyday errands.
